# Menu photograph provenance

**These are stand-in images, not Bukhara's food.** Each was matched to a dish by
name, downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and uploaded to this project's own
public Storage bucket at `menu/<itemId>.jpg`. They are hosted by us rather than
hotlinked, so no external site is loaded by a guest's browser.

They are reusable, but every file carries its own licence and several require
attribution. **Before launch, either replace all of these with the restaurant's
own photography, or check each licence and add the required credits.**

A few are approximate rather than exact: `garlic-naan` reuses the naan
photograph, `beef-masala` reuses a mutton curry, and `classic-dagwood` reuses a
burger. Re-run with `scripts/upload-menu-images.ts` after replacing the files.
